Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Click the "Create Listing" button in the hero section or navigation menu. Fill in the required details including title, description, price, and category. You can choose between a one-time product sale or a recurring service.

Auctions allow sellers to list items with a starting price and end time. Buyers can place bids, and the highest bidder at the end wins. You can also set up auto-bidding to automatically bid up to your maximum amount.

We currently support cryptocurrency payments including TON and USDT. Connect your wallet in the settings to start buying and selling.

Services are listings marked as "recurring". Click on the service, view the seller's availability, and click "Book Service" to select a time slot. The seller will receive your booking request.
